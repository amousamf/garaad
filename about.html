Garaad Play

Maths and English games for children aged 3 to 12

Garaad is free. It runs on any phone, at home or in the classroom, and keeps working without internet.

In Somali, garaad means wisdom.

Open the games

What children do

Maths

31 games covering numbers, adding and taking away, shapes and measures, patterns and logic, and problem solving. Every game has 10 levels.

English

21 games that start with listening and sounds, then words and letters, then reading and writing. A Word Garden grows as children learn new words.

The right level

A short quick check places each child as an Explorer (about 3–5), Builder (6–8) or Thinker (9–12), whatever their age.

Made for children who are still learning English

Most children who use Garaad speak Somali at home. Little ones learn English by listening and speaking: a voice reads every question, and key words are repeated. Older children move on to reading and writing.

Wrong answers are explained with pictures, missed questions come back later for another try, and a 10-minute "Today's practice" mixes review with something new.

For families

Open garaad.app on a phone, add each child with their name and age, and the right games appear. There are no parent accounts and no adverts.

For schools

Teachers log in with a school code and their phone number. They pick a class and a child, and each child's progress follows them on any school phone. Results are saved even when there is no internet and are sent later.

Garaad is being tried in schools now. If you would like to use it in your school, get in touch.
