# Sound credits

The pure letter sounds in `s/`, `r/` and `k/` are cut from recordings on Wikimedia Commons (the recordings used on Wikipedia's IPA pages).
Each file's licence and author are on its Commons page, linked below. Most are public domain or CC BY-SA; derived sounds keep the same licence.

Joined sounds (made by joining two of these recordings): ai = e + i, oa = er + oo, ie = a + i, ou = a + oo, oi = or + i, ue = y + oo, x = c + s, qu = c + w.

The example words (`w/`) and made-up words (`x/`) are made with the Kokoro voice (Apache 2.0).
