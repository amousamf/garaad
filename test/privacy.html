Privacy

Last updated 25 October 2026

The short version

  • We keep only what helps children learn: a first name, an age and how they do in the games. For a family account, we also keep the parent's name, WhatsApp number and where they live.
  • No adverts, no tracking tools, and we never sell information.
  • Children never chat with anyone and do not have accounts. A grown-up sets everything up.
  • You can ask us to delete a child's information at any time.

Who runs Garaad

Garaad is run by Abdifatah, who built it. Questions or requests about information go to muse.afatah@gmail.com.

At home (family phones)

When a grown-up adds a child, the child's name and age and their progress (levels, stars and missed questions) are saved on the phone itself.

They are also saved to the family's account in a secure database (Supabase), so the children's progress follows them to another phone and we can see how children are getting on and make the games better. The account holds:

  • each child's name, age, owl and colours, and their progress
  • each level played: the game, the level, how many answers were right first time, stars, hints, missed questions, and the date and time
  • how long each play session lasted

Only the family (with their WhatsApp number and PIN) and Garaad's owner, as the administrator, can see a family's children. Before October 2026, family results went to a private Google Sheet that only Garaad's owner can open; it is kept as an archive and no longer receives anything.

Family accounts

A family account is free. When a parent registers, the secure database (Supabase) keeps:

  • the parent's name and WhatsApp number
  • their country, region and the location they type (for example, a district)
  • whether they asked to join the Garaad parents' WhatsApp group
  • their 4-digit PIN, stored scrambled so nobody can read it

The number and PIN let the family open Garaad on another phone. Only Garaad's owner, as the administrator, can see families' details. We use the WhatsApp number to help with the account and, only if the parent ticked the box, to add them to the parents' group. Parents' numbers and locations are never included when we share results.

At school

In school mode, information is kept in a secure database (Supabase) so that a child's progress follows them on any school phone. It holds:

  • the school and class, and each child's name, age and the group the teacher expects (for example, "medium")
  • the child's starting exercise score, the levels they play and their results
  • each teacher's name and phone number, and their PIN, which is stored scrambled so nobody can read it
  • the owl and colours each child picks, hints they buy with stars, and, if the teacher adds it, a parent's phone number for the weekly progress report
  • a short code for each child, used instead of their name when results are shared

Teachers see the children at their own school. The principal can view but not change anything. Garaad's owner, as the administrator, manages schools and codes. Results played without internet wait on the phone and are sent later.

The microphone

Two speaking games use the microphone, and only after the phone asks for permission.

  • Parrot Echo records a few seconds, plays it back in a parrot voice, then throws it away. The recording never leaves the phone.
  • Name It! uses the phone's own speech recognition to check the word a child says. On many phones, for example Chrome on Android, the phone's browser sends that short piece of sound to Google to turn it into text. Garaad does not keep the sound or the text. A grown-up can always mark the answer instead.

Other services

Like any website, the services that deliver Garaad see basic technical details such as the phone's internet address. These are GitHub (which hosts the app), Cloudflare (which runs the garaad.app address) and Google Fonts (which supplies the lettering). Garaad does not use them to track anyone.

Sharing results

We do not sell or give away children's information. If we share how the games are working, for example with a school's leaders or with organisations that support the project, we remove children's names first.

Removing information

  • Families: remove a child in the Parent area. This deletes the child and all their results from the phone and the database. Email us to delete the whole family account, or old rows in the archived Sheet.
  • Schools: ask the school or email us, and we will delete the child's records from the database.

Changes

If what Garaad keeps changes, for example when new features are added, we will update this page and the date at the top.
